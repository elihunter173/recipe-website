《悯农》李绅

锄禾曰当午，

汗滴禾下土。

谁知盘中餐，

粒粒皆辛苦。

“Feel for the Farmer” by Li Shen (translated by Eli Hunter)

Hoeing the fields at noon,

sweat dripping from grain to ground.

Who appreciates on every plate,

each grain of rice is such hard work?
